# SmartFlow — Final Report

A multi-agent digital twin for adaptive traffic signal control, built on SUMO and benchmarked against classical baselines over twelve weeks.

Generated by `src/final_report.py` from the committed artifacts: 78 Python files, 19,774 lines, 19 commits.

## 1. What was built

A 12-junction signalised corridor in SUMO, and five layers on top of it:

| Layer | What it does | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Benchmark harness | Fixed-time and actuated baselines, one per-second metric collector shared by every controller | 1 |
| Reinforcement learning | PPO on a standard benchmark, then one junction, then all twelve with parameter sharing, green-wave shaping and a Lagrangian fairness constraint | 2–6 |
| Knowledge and retrieval | A 301-node knowledge graph over Neo4j, Chroma retrieval over the project's own reports, and a read-only answering service that refuses out-of-domain questions | 7 |
| Perception and planning | A vehicle detector, a causal incident detector, and a closure/weather scenario planner | 8 |
| Federation and language | Federated averaging across districts, a LoRA adapter on synthetic traffic Q&A, and emergency-vehicle preemption | 9 |
| Platform | Five FastAPI services behind bearer authentication, Redpanda events, Prometheus/Grafana/Loki, a Helm chart on k3d, and a Next.js dashboard | 10–12 |

## 2. The headline result

Corridor-wide, base demand, 3-seed means. Every controller runs the same traffic and is measured by the same collector.

| Controller | Avg wait (s) | Max queue | Throughput | CO2 (kg) | Seeds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `actuated` | 40.89 | 76 | 1647 | 508.3 | 3 |
| `fixed` | 83.10 | 428 | 1280 | 732.6 | 3 |
| `marl_independent` | 17.80 | 41 | 1680 | 451.0 | 3 |
| `marl_shared_w5` | 17.85 | 150 | 1569 | 474.7 | 3 |
| `marl_shared_w5gnn` | 18.16 | 42 | 1679 | 452.0 | 3 |
| `marl_shared_w5nocoord` | 18.16 | 42 | 1679 | 452.0 | 3 |

Reinforcement learning cuts average wait from **83.1 s** (fixed-time) and **40.9 s** (actuated) to **17.8 s** — 79% below fixed-time and 56% below a genuinely strong classical baseline.

## 3. What failed, and why that matters

Three Definitions of Done were not met. All three are reported as failures rather than re-scoped until they passed.

| Week | Definition of Done | Why it failed |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | RL beats both baselines on ≥2 of 3 metrics at one junction | Beats fixed-time on all three, but the actuated baseline on only one. One junction of twelve also barely moves the corridor. |
| 5 | Corridor RL beats both baselines on wait **and** throughput; fairness caps worst-case wait | Beats actuated on wait, but one seed of three converged to a policy that jams the corridor and drags throughput below baseline. The fairness constraint changed nothing measurable. |
| 9 | Federated averaging improves a held-out district | Improves it by exactly 0.0%. |

### The one finding that explains two of them

Five shaped policy variants in Week 5 produced byte-identical metrics. Week 6 measured why: they agree on **100% of the decisions that can actually move a signal**. Week 9 then found the same thing from an independent direction — three districts, trained separately on their own traffic, produce identical wait times on a held-out junction.

That was instrumented rather than assumed. Mean L2 distance between district weight vectors is **1.41** — they are genuinely different networks — while action agreement on the held-out junction is **100.0%**. Distinct weights, identical behaviour.

**The cause is the action space, not any of the algorithms.** With a binary next-phase action and a 5-second minimum green, almost every reasonable policy collapses onto the same greedy rule. Reward shaping changes what a policy believes without changing what it does, and federated averaging has nothing to average. The fix is a finer action space — phase-duration control — and it is the single clearest next piece of work in this project.

## 4. The rest of the results

**Vehicle detection.** YOLOv8n reaches mAP50 **0.988** and recall **0.982** on junction C2, held out of training entirely (4,958 unseen boxes). Trained on frames rendered from the twin with labels derived from TraCI ground truth.

**Incident detection.** Recall **1.00** on injected lane obstructions across 3 seeds, with **30 s** mean detection latency, using per-lane causal z-score detectors.

**Emergency preemption.** Emergency-vehicle wait falls from **416 s** to **58 s** (86% lower) with general traffic unharmed (-0.6% wait).

**LoRA fine-tune.** Held-out perplexity falls from **13.24** to **1.83**, training 294,912 of 82,207,488 parameters.

## 5. The platform

Five FastAPI services — simulation, RL, vision, graph and analytics — each serving the project's committed artifacts over HTTP behind bearer authentication. They run two ways from the same images: Docker Compose, and a Helm chart on a k3d Kubernetes cluster. Both were brought up and verified; `tests/test_services.py` runs 12 end-to-end checks against a live stack.

Two architectural rules are enforced by tests rather than by convention:

- **The analytics service can never touch the control loop.** It imports nothing from the simulation or RL side, and its OpenAPI schema is checked for mutating routes.
- **Authentication is not optional.** Every protected route is called without a token and must return 401. The development token mode is refused outright when `SMARTFLOW_ENV=production`.

## 6. Honest limitations

These bound every number above.

- **One synthetic network.** All results come from a single 12-junction grid with a synthetic demand generator. Nothing here demonstrates transfer to a real road network; the OSM corridor has been deferred since Week 1.
- **Three seeds.** Enough to show variance, not enough for confidence intervals.
- **The detector has never seen a real camera.** mAP50 ≈ 0.99 is measured on rendered overhead frames with no occlusion, perspective, motion blur or lens weather. UA-DETRAC, which would have provided real footage, is behind a registration wall and was not used.
- **The language model is not Phi-3.** A CPU-only machine cannot fine-tune 3.8B parameters, so a much smaller model stands in. Nothing here is a result about Phi-3-mini.
- **Nothing has been load-tested.** No claim is made anywhere in this project about real-time or production performance.
- **The saturated action space limits what any of the learning results can show.** Until the action space is refined, shaping and federation cannot be evaluated properly on this corridor.
